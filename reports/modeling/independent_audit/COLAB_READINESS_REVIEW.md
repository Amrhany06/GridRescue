**P1 scientific correction before expansion.** Tier B and C simultaneously change count, county/state composition, class balance, calendar representation, number of one-hot inputs, and the population selecting the operating cutoff. A better AP at B would support the *combined* intervention. For separate attribution add controls in `experiment_manifest.json`, `cloud_data.py::expanded` and `cloud_train.py::{inputs,train,evaluate}`: (1) a fixed diverse county set subsampled to 1M and 3M within common state/month strata, (2) a narrow versus broad county mix at the same feasible row count, and (3) a shared original-seen OOF policy panel for all variants. Sample whole county-time blocks reproducibly from already purged fit partitions, never from validation; keep all selected counties represented where feasible. Use paired block uncertainty and state tables on identical pilot rows. Unit tests must show reproducible nesting, disjoint outer states, a fixed evaluation digest and no future dates in fit blocks. If only the original tiers are affordable, label the comparison a combined breadth-and-volume intervention and forgo a size-only claim. Additional controls require new fits. No reason to rerun historical local experiments.

**Decision to spend.** Freeze E1/E2 endpoints, roles and comparison policy before looking at any new results. Approve B only after A clears parity. Consider C after B has sufficient resource headroom and its paired evaluation has been recorded under the frozen stopping rule. Reporting unfavorable results at B is part of the experiment. Limit any national-generalization claim until several unseen states have been evaluated.
