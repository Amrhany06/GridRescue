# Experiments and promotion decisions

These are proposed implementation specifications for a separately approved development run. Paths below are relative to the repository. Preserve the current pipeline and benchmark under their existing identities. The unit of comparison is (five-character FIPS, UTC timestamp), with equal labels required whenever the task definition is unchanged.

## Common comparison contract

Freeze the outer time/state splits, sample eligibility, candidate list, primary endpoint and fallback rules before generating candidate scores. For each candidate retain the same anchor manifest, fold-fitted preprocessing, estimator settings and compute budget except for the variable under test. Fit calibration and choose decision rules on earlier out-of-fold predictions only. For temporal tests use later periods; for spatial transfer learn every supervised statistic outside the held-out state. Persist full-precision row scores, identities, split boundaries, version hashes and cutoffs. Report micro and state-macro AP, class prevalence, precision at descriptive 55% and 60% recall, ROC AUC, actual recall/precision and FP per 1,000 at the frozen policy, Brier score and reliability by state. Keep 2021 a development benchmark.

**Proposed ranking promotion gate.** In paired outer predictions require positive lower 95% calendar-block bounds on both the primary AP difference and precision difference at 55% matched recall. At the frozen operating policy require actual recall within two percentage points of the control or higher and no increase in FP/1,000. For a geographic improvement claim also require a positive mean state AP difference, favorable AP differences in at least three of four prespecified held-out states, and worst-state AP loss ≤0.01. Predeclare one primary candidate comparison per stage or adjust for multiple comparisons. These tolerances are suggested design decisions awaiting owner approval. An inconclusive interval warrants reporting uncertainty. Calibration-only and notification policies have different endpoints below.

Use paired calendar weeks spanning all counties per replicate, 2,000 replicates for the planned comparison, and repeat with 14-day blocks. Include per-county estimates and equal-county macro summaries. For a claim about new states, report variation across outer states separately; resampling time within one state cannot establish that claim.

**Paper extensions after the primary evaluation.** Register a single estimand and analysis plan. Compare existing lookup baselines, past-trend/persistence heuristics and a regularized logistic model with identical geography/time covariates. Evaluate each on exactly the same outer rows. Include several prospectively selected unseen states, year-to-year comparisons, uncertainty with dependence-preserving resampling, and sensitivity to weighting counties equally. Link a consented/authorized independent sample of utility incidents or timestamped reports to measure match rate, lag, onset and disagreement by county and coverage. Specify linkage tolerance before reviewing discrepancies. A claim about early physical warning needs lead time relative to that independent onset and a reported unmatched fraction. Additional model families alone cannot answer that question.
